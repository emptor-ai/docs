# Core concepts

> How the Supplier API's main resources relate

## Work orders

A **work order** is a request for service dispatched to your facility. It has a buyer, a location, one or more assets, a trade, and a status. You accept it, schedule service calls against it, and eventually invoice for it.

## Service calls

A **service call** is a scheduled visit against a work order. One work order can have many service calls (e.g. diagnostic, repair, follow-up). Each service call records who went, when, and what happened.

## Quotes

**Quotes** are your estimates for a work order, submitted for buyer approval before work proceeds.

## Invoices

**Invoices** are the final bill for completed work. Usually one per work order.

## WrenchMode

**WrenchMode** captures per-technician work and drive time. The analytics endpoint rolls it up per technician over a window (capped at one month); the raw events endpoint returns the underlying event log.

## Purchasing and inventory

The purchasing endpoints track purchase requests, purchase orders, receipts, and the parts you consume on work orders.
