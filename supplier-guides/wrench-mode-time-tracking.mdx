# WrenchMode time tracking

> Pull per-technician work and drive time

WrenchMode exposes two endpoints:

- `GET /v1/supplier/wrench_mode/events/analytics/{fromDate}/{toDate}` — per-technician rollup of drive time, work time, and total time. Window capped at one month.
- `GET /v1/supplier/wrench_mode/events` — the raw event log the rollup is calculated from.

## Choosing between them

- Use the **analytics** endpoint for dashboards and payroll reports where you need a single number per technician per period.
- Use the **raw events** endpoint when you need per-visit reconstruction or want to audit how the rollup was calculated.

## Correlating with service calls

The service-call endpoints have `/with_work_logs` and `/with_tech_details` expansions that give you the per-visit story alongside WrenchMode events.
