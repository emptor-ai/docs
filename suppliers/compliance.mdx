Your customers require current compliance documentation — insurance certificates, licenses, and any customer-specific requirements — before work can route to you. OpenWrench tracks it all in one place, per customer.

## How it works

- **Upload once, tracked always** — documents live on your supplier profile with their expiration dates.
- **Renewal reminders** — you're prompted before documents expire, with time to get renewals from your broker.
- **Customer visibility** — customers see your compliance status, not your documents' contents beyond what they require.

## Why it matters operationally

A lapsed insurance certificate can pause you from receiving dispatches until it's current — usually at the worst possible moment. Treat renewal reminders like the revenue protection they are.

<Tip>
  Put your office manager or bookkeeper on compliance notifications. The person who talks to your insurance broker should be the one who hears a certificate is 30 days from expiring.
</Tip>

## Different customers, different requirements

Each customer can require different documents or coverage levels. Your profile tracks compliance per customer relationship, so meeting one customer's higher bar doesn't get lost when another asks for the same thing.
