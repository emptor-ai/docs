OpenWrench includes inventory management for your parts and equipment — so what's on the truck, what's in the shop, and what went into a job stay accounted for.

## What you can track

- **Parts catalog** — the parts you stock and use, with units of measure and cost
- **Stock levels** — quantities across your stock locations (shop, warehouse, trucks)
- **Usage on jobs** — parts consumed against service calls, so job costing and invoicing line up with reality
- **Equipment** — tools and equipment your teams carry

## Why it's worth using inside OpenWrench

Parts logged against the service call flow into the work order record — the same record your invoice bills against. When a part shows up on an invoice, the customer can see it was used on the job. Fewer questions, faster approvals.

It also answers your own operational questions: what parts do we burn the most, what should live on the trucks, and what's sitting on a shelf tying up cash.

<Tip>
  Start with your high-turn parts — the filters, belts, and fittings you touch weekly — rather than cataloging everything. Inventory systems earn trust through the parts people actually look up.
</Tip>
