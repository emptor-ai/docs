Every customer you serve on OpenWrench sees your performance record — built automatically from the work itself, not from surveys. You see the same numbers in your portal, which means no surprises in a quarterly review.

## The KPIs that matter

| KPI | What drives it |
| --- | --- |
| Acceptance time | How fast you respond to new dispatches |
| Time to schedule / time to arrive | How quickly committed work gets a date, and how soon that date is |
| ETA adherence | Showing up inside the window you committed — or updating early when you can't |
| First-time fix | Jobs resolved in one visit vs. repeat visits for the same problem |
| Cost discipline | Invoices vs. NTE and quotes; how often you need increases |
| Documentation quality | Check-ins, work logs, and photos on your service calls |

## Using your record to win work

Dispatchers see performance when they choose suppliers — and automated dispatch weighs it too. A strong record is a structural advantage: it wins you tie-breaks every day, and it's evidence you can bring to conversations about rates, coverage expansion, or new customers on the platform.

## If a number looks wrong

Every KPI traces back to specific work orders. If something looks off — a no-show that was actually a customer cancellation, say — the work order record is where the story lives. This is another reason to document reschedules and access issues on the thread: the record defends you.

<Tip>
  The two fastest levers on your record: accept work within the hour, and never let an ETA slip silently. Both are habits, not investments.
</Tip>
