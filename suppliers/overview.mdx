If one of your customers runs their facilities on OpenWrench, this is your side of the platform: where work arrives, where you schedule and document it, where you quote, and where you invoice and get paid.

**OpenWrench is free for suppliers.** There are no subscription fees, no per-work-order charges, and no percentage taken from your invoices. Full stop.

## Why suppliers like working this way

- **Complete work orders** — photos, asset history, and access notes before you roll a truck means fewer wasted trips.
- **Fast approvals** — NTE increases and quotes route to approvers who can see your evidence, instead of dying in an inbox.
- **Payment visibility** — you see exactly where every invoice stands, without calling accounts payable.
- **A fair shot at more work** — your performance record speaks for itself to every customer you serve on the platform.
