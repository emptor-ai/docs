# Core concepts

## Companies, facilities, and contacts

Your API key is issued to a **contact**, who belongs to a **facility**, which rolls up to a **company**. Every request is scoped to that company.

## Locations and assets

- **Locations** are the physical sites you operate.
- **Assets** are pieces of equipment attached to a location.

Work orders reference both.

## Work orders

A **work order** is a request for service. It has a location, one or more assets, a trade, a status, and an assigned supplier (once dispatched). Work orders progress through statuses as suppliers accept, schedule, execute, and invoice work.

## Suppliers

Suppliers are the service providers who fulfill work orders. Your supplier network is available through the supplier endpoints; dispatching a work order assigns it to one of them.

## Quotes and invoices

- **Quotes** are supplier estimates you approve before work proceeds.
- **Invoices** are the final bill for completed work.

## Preventive maintenance (PM)

PM schedules generate recurring work orders on assets at a defined cadence.
