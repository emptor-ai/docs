Not everything is a single repair. Two structures handle bigger-than-one-work-order efforts.

## Projects

A **project** groups related work orders under one umbrella — a remodel, a rollout across locations, a seasonal readiness push. The project view shows overall progress, spend against the project, and which pieces are stuck, while each work order underneath runs its normal lifecycle (dispatch, service calls, completion, cost).

Internal teams often anchor projects: the in-house crew does the coordinated work, with specialist suppliers pulled in for specific work orders inside the same project.

## Site surveys and walkthroughs

**Site surveys** are structured location assessments — walking a site against a defined checklist and recording condition, findings, and photos. Use them for:

- **Condition assessments** — periodic health checks of a location and its equipment
- **New location onboarding** — capturing the as-built reality: assets, areas, access details
- **Brand standards audits** — verifying sites against the standard, with photo evidence
- **Seasonal readiness** — pre-summer HVAC walks, pre-winter roof and heating checks

Findings don't stay in the report: survey items convert into work orders, assigned in-house or to suppliers, each traceable back to the survey that raised it.

<Tip>
  Surveys are also how asset data gets good. A tech with the checklist standing in front of the unit is the cheapest data-quality moment you'll ever get — confirm the nameplate, snap the photo, correct the record.
</Tip>
