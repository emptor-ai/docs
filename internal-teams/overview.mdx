These docs are for **internal service teams** — the in-house technicians, handymen, and self-perform crews who do maintenance work as employees of the organization, not as third-party vendors. If your company runs its facilities on OpenWrench and you fix things for a living, this is your section.

Internal teams work inside the organization's own account, with the same work order backbone the rest of the company uses — plus field workflows built for employees: WrenchMode check-in/check-out, purchase requests for parts and materials, and internal inventory.

## Internal teams vs. suppliers

The difference in one sentence: suppliers quote and invoice; internal teams check in, do the work, and request what they need to buy. Both leave the same kind of trail on the work order — which is what lets the organization decide, with real numbers, what stays in-house versus what goes to the network.
