Your **private network** is the set of suppliers you've onboarded and approved to serve your locations. Building it well — the right trades, in the right places, with clear expectations — is most of what makes dispatch fast.

## What a supplier record holds

- **Trades and sub-trades** — the specific skills they cover, from OpenWrench's structured trade taxonomy (plumbing, electrical, refrigeration, HVAC, and dozens more with finer sub-trades)
- **Coverage** — which of your locations or geographies they serve
- **Compliance status** — insurance certificates, licenses, and documents, with expirations tracked
- **Rates and terms** — what you've agreed to
- **Performance history** — how they've actually done for you (see [Supplier Performance](/buyers/suppliers/performance))

## Onboarding suppliers

Suppliers join your network **at no cost to them** — OpenWrench never charges suppliers to participate, which matters when you're recruiting good vendors who've been burned by pay-to-play platforms. A supplier you invite sets up their profile, declares trades and coverage, uploads compliance documents, and can start receiving work.

<Note>
  VFA vendor onboarding can run this process for you — chasing documents, verifying coverage, and getting new suppliers dispatch-ready without your team project-managing it.
</Note>

## Finding coverage gaps

At portfolio scale the question isn't "do we have a plumber?" but "do we have a plumber for _every_ site — including the new market we just opened?" OpenWrench shows network coverage by trade and geography, so gaps surface before a 2am emergency finds them for you.

## Sourcing events (RFPs)

When you need new suppliers or want to re-bid existing work, **sourcing events** run a structured RFP inside the platform: define the scope, invite suppliers, collect bids in comparable form, and award. Awarded suppliers flow straight into your network with terms recorded — no email attachments, no spreadsheet reconciliation.

## Compliance monitoring

Insurance lapses are a real liability. OpenWrench tracks compliance document expirations across your network and flags suppliers who fall out of compliance so they can be paused from dispatch until documents are current.
