Because every service call, ETA, quote, and invoice runs through OpenWrench, supplier performance isn't a survey — it's a byproduct of the work itself.

## What gets measured

Performance draws on the operational record:

- **Responsiveness** — time to accept work, time to schedule, time to arrive
- **Reliability** — ETA adherence, no-shows, reschedules
- **First-time fix** — how often one visit resolves the problem vs. repeat visits for the same issue
- **Cost discipline** — invoice amounts vs. NTE and quotes, frequency of NTE increase requests
- **Compliance** — documentation currency, completion evidence quality

## Where performance data shows up

- **At dispatch** — dispatchers see performance alongside trade and coverage match, so the best available supplier wins the job, not the most familiar one
- **In supplier reviews** — quarterly conversations grounded in shared data rather than anecdotes
- **In network decisions** — who gets more locations, who gets re-bid, who gets removed

<Tip>
  Share scorecards with your suppliers. The same transparency that helps you manage the network helps good suppliers prove they're good — and gives underperformers a concrete picture of what to fix. Suppliers see their own KPIs in their portal.
</Tip>

## Fair comparisons

Context matters when comparing suppliers: emergency-heavy trades will always show different response profiles than planned-work trades, and a supplier covering rural sites carries different drive times than a metro one. Compare within trade and territory before acting on rankings.
