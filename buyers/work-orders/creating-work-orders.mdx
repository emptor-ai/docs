Work orders can be created by anyone your organization allows — facilities managers, store or location staff, or automatically from PM schedules and integrations.

## Creating a work order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the location and problem type">
    Choose where the issue is and what kind of problem it is. The problem type determines the trade needed and drives which suppliers are suggested for dispatch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the issue">
    Add a description, photos, and the affected asset if known. Tying the work order to an asset builds service history and helps技 the supplier arrive prepared.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set priority and NTE">
    Set the urgency and the Not-To-Exceed amount the supplier can spend without further approval. Many organizations preset NTEs by problem type so requesters don't have to decide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dispatch">
    Assign a supplier from your network or your internal team — or let dispatch rules and the Virtual Facilities Assistant handle assignment. See [Dispatch & Assignment](/buyers/work-orders/dispatch).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting before you dispatch

Not every problem needs a truck roll. OpenWrench supports **troubleshooting flows** tied to problem types — guided steps a requester walks through before a work order is dispatched (check the breaker, verify the thermostat setting, confirm the unit is plugged in). Resolved-on-the-spot issues never become dispatches, which saves real money at scale.

<Tip>
  Deflected work orders are tracked, so you can see how much troubleshooting is saving you — both in avoided dispatch fees and in downtime.
</Tip>

## What good work orders capture

The more context captured at creation, the fewer follow-up trips:

- **Photos and video** of the problem
- **The specific asset**, not just "the HVAC"
- **Access notes** — hours, parking, who to ask for on site
- **Priority** that reflects actual business impact (a down walk-in is not the same as a flickering light)

## Where work orders come from

Beyond manual creation, work orders are generated by:

- **PM schedules** — recurring maintenance generates work orders automatically
- **Site surveys and walkthroughs** — findings convert into work orders
- **Customer-reported requests** — email-based intake for locations that report issues by email
- **The Virtual Facilities Assistant** — issues surfaced through VFA channels
