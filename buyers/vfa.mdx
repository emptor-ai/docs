Most facilities work isn't decisions — it's follow-through. Chasing an ETA, nudging an approver, confirming a PM got scheduled, checking why an invoice is late. The **Virtual Facilities Assistant (VFA)** is OpenWrench's managed operational layer that does that follow-through for you, inside the guardrails you set.

VFA operates across eight capability areas:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Dispatch">
    Assigns new work orders to the right supplier based on trade, coverage, and performance — and keeps working the assignment until a supplier has accepted and scheduled.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="ETA oversight">
    Watches every committed ETA, follows up when one slips or a supplier goes quiet, and escalates to your team only when a human decision is needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="PM support">
    Keeps preventive maintenance on track — following up on overdue PMs, confirming scheduling, and protecting your compliance numbers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="RFP management">
    Runs sourcing events end to end: inviting suppliers, chasing bids, and getting responses into comparable shape for your decision.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Post-dispatch support">
    Handles the long tail after assignment — reschedules, access issues, parts delays — keeping the work order moving and the record current.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Vendor onboarding">
    Gets new suppliers from invited to dispatch-ready: profile setup, trades and coverage, compliance documents collected and verified.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="NTE oversight">
    Watches pending NTE increase requests so suppliers aren't stuck waiting on site, and nudges approvers before delays cost money.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Invoice monitoring">
    Watches invoice patterns across the account — late submissions, NTE-ceiling billing, creep — and flags what deserves review.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## You set the guardrails

VFA works within rules your team defines: which suppliers are preferred, what NTEs apply, when to escalate to a human, and which decisions are never automated. Everything VFA does is recorded on the work order like any other actor's activity — fully visible, fully auditable.
