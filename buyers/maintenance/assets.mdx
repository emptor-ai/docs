Assets are the equipment your locations depend on — HVAC units, refrigeration, kitchen equipment, fitness machines, boilers. In OpenWrench, an asset is more than an inventory line: it's the anchor that connects every work order, PM, quote, and invoice about a piece of equipment into one history.

## The asset record

Each asset carries:

- **Identity** — type, make, model, serial number, and custom fields your organization defines
- **Location and area** — where it lives, down to the area within the site
- **Warranty details** — so covered repairs don't get paid for twice
- **Service history** — every work order, service call, and invoice tied to the asset
- **Documents and photos** — manuals, install records, nameplate shots

## Why the work-order link matters

When work orders are tied to assets, questions that are usually unanswerable become routine:

- What has this unit cost us over its life?
- Which locations' equipment fails most — and is it age, usage, or maintenance?
- Is this the same compressor failing for the third time this year?

That history is exactly what an informed repair-vs-replace decision needs, and it's what [quote review](/buyers/spend/quotes) puts in front of approvers.

## Provisioning at scale

Opening or refreshing locations doesn't mean manual data entry. Assets can be **provisioned in bulk from brand-level asset groups** — define the standard equipment package for a location type once, then stamp it out per site. Site surveys and walkthroughs are the natural moments to true-up asset data against reality.

<Tip>
  Asset data quality is a flywheel: technicians who find accurate asset records keep them accurate; technicians who find junk stop looking. Start with your highest-spend equipment categories — usually HVAC and refrigeration — rather than trying to catalog everything at once.
</Tip>
