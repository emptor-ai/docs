OpenWrench is an all-in-one facilities management platform for multi-site operators — restaurant groups, fitness chains, healthcare facilities, retail, and commercial property teams. It manages the full life of a repair or maintenance request: from the moment a problem is reported at a location, through dispatch, on-site work, approvals, and invoicing.

What sets OpenWrench apart is that it covers **both sides of your maintenance operation** in one place: third-party service providers _and_ your own in-house technicians. Every conversation, ETA, quote, approval, and invoice lives on the work order itself — so nobody has to chase updates over email or phone to know what's happening.

## How the platform is organized

OpenWrench serves three audiences, and these docs are organized the same way:

- **Buyers** (this section) — facility, operations, and finance teams at the organizations that own or operate locations.
- [**Suppliers**](/suppliers/overview) — third-party service providers who receive and perform work. Suppliers use OpenWrench for free.
- [**Internal Teams**](/internal-teams/overview) — your own in-house maintenance technicians and self-perform teams, who work inside your organization's account.
